Kankurgachhi Jubak Brinda

Kankurgachhi Jubak Brinda is a Durga Puja pandal in Kankurgachi, Kolkata. Near C.I.T. Road, Kankurgachi. Girish Park metro is 1.6 km away, about 30 min on foot.

How to reach Kankurgachhi Jubak Brinda

Take the metro to Girish Park and leave by Gate 3, the exit nearest the pandal. From there it is 1.6 km on foot, about 30 minutes (measured in a straight line; the street walk is a little longer).

On Puja evenings the police close roads and turn some one way; follow the barricades on the ground.

When to visit Kankurgachhi Jubak Brinda

Durga Puja 2026 runs from Shashthi on Friday 16 October to Bijoya Dashami on Wednesday 21 October; Mahalaya is Saturday 10 October.

Across the city, evenings from Saptami to Navami are the busiest; mornings and the days before Saptami are quieter.
