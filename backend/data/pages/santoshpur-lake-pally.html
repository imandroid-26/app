How to reach Santoshpur Lake Pally

Take the metro to Satyajit Ray and leave by Gate 2, the exit nearest the pandal. From there it is 1.9 km on foot, about 29 minutes along the streets.

On Puja evenings the police close roads and turn some one way; follow the barricades on the ground.

Santoshpur Lake Pally Durga Puja 2026 theme

The 2026 theme is Tandav, Shiva's cosmic dance, as reported by Bartaman.

When to visit Santoshpur Lake Pally

Durga Puja 2026 runs from Shashthi on Friday 16 October to Bijoya Dashami on Wednesday 21 October; Mahalaya is Saturday 10 October.

Santoshpur Lake Pally is one of the big pujas: Evenings are busiest, when the lights come on, and Saptami to Navami most of all; expect a queue, with separate lines for men and women. For the shortest wait, come in the morning.
