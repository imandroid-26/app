How to reach Netajigarh Nagarik Brinda

Take the metro to Howrah Maidan. From there it is 3.1 km on foot, about 58 minutes (measured in a straight line; the street walk is a little longer).

On Puja evenings the police close roads and turn some one way; follow the barricades on the ground.

When to visit Netajigarh Nagarik Brinda

Durga Puja 2026 runs from Shashthi on Friday 16 October to Bijoya Dashami on Wednesday 21 October; Mahalaya is Saturday 10 October.

Across the city, evenings from Saptami to Navami are the busiest; mornings and the days before Saptami are quieter.
