How to reach Laketown Adhibashibrindo

Take the metro to Belgachia and leave by Gate 3, the exit nearest the pandal. From there it is 1.8 km on foot, about 34 minutes (measured in a straight line; the street walk is a little longer).

Laketown Adhibashibrindo is also a stop on a ready-made walking route, which takes you on to the next pandals in order.

On Puja evenings the police close roads and turn some one way; follow the barricades on the ground.

When to visit Laketown Adhibashibrindo

Durga Puja 2026 runs from Shashthi on Friday 16 October to Bijoya Dashami on Wednesday 21 October; Mahalaya is Saturday 10 October.

Across the city, evenings from Saptami to Navami are the busiest; mornings and the days before Saptami are quieter.
