How to reach FD Block Durga Puja

Take the metro to City Centre. From there it is 560 m on foot, about 10 minutes (measured in a straight line; the street walk is a little longer).

FD Block Durga Puja is also a stop on a ready-made walking route, which takes you on to the next pandals in order.

On Puja evenings the police close roads and turn some one way; follow the barricades on the ground.

When to visit FD Block Durga Puja

Durga Puja 2026 runs from Shashthi on Friday 16 October to Bijoya Dashami on Wednesday 21 October; Mahalaya is Saturday 10 October.

FD Block Durga Puja is one of the big pujas: Evenings are busiest, when the lights come on, and Saptami to Navami most of all; expect a queue, with separate lines for men and women. For the shortest wait, come in the morning.
