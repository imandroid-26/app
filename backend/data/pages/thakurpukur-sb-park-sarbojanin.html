How to reach Thakurpukur SB Park Sarbojanin

Take the metro to Thakurpukur. From there it is 410 m on foot, about 8 minutes (measured in a straight line; the street walk is a little longer).

On Puja evenings the police close roads and turn some one way; follow the barricades on the ground.

Thakurpukur SB Park Sarbojanin Durga Puja 2026 theme

The 2026 theme is Colliery: Durga in a coal mine, the miners' labour as her shakti, as reported by ETV Bharat.

When to visit Thakurpukur SB Park Sarbojanin

Durga Puja 2026 runs from Shashthi on Friday 16 October to Bijoya Dashami on Wednesday 21 October; Mahalaya is Saturday 10 October.

Thakurpukur SB Park Sarbojanin is one of the big pujas: Evenings are busiest, when the lights come on, and Saptami to Navami most of all; expect a queue, with separate lines for men and women. For the shortest wait, come in the morning.
