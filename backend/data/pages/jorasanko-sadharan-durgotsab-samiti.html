How to reach Jorasanko Sadharan Durgotsab Samiti

Take the metro to Girish Park and leave by Gate 1, the exit nearest the pandal. From there it is 370 m on foot, about 7 minutes (measured in a straight line; the street walk is a little longer).

Jorasanko Sadharan Durgotsab Samiti is also a stop on a ready-made walking route, which takes you on to the next pandals in order.

On Puja evenings the police close roads and turn some one way; follow the barricades on the ground.

When to visit Jorasanko Sadharan Durgotsab Samiti

Durga Puja 2026 runs from Shashthi on Friday 16 October to Bijoya Dashami on Wednesday 21 October; Mahalaya is Saturday 10 October.

Across the city, evenings from Saptami to Navami are the busiest; mornings and the days before Saptami are quieter.
